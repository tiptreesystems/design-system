Hairline

--tt-border-hairline is 1px, and 0.5px under @media (resolution >= 192dpi) in primitives.css and tokens.css. The value is the token's; how an engine rasterises half a pixel is its own.

Icons

Every glyph of @tiptree/design-system/icons.js, drawn at 16 and 20 in both themes from dist/icons/icons.json. The line is 1.5px at both sizes because every stroked shape carries vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"; consumers set size and colour only.
